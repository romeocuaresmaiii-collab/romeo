What do the marketing words mean?

Hook — the first sentence or visual that catches attention.

Caption — the text written with a post.

CTA — the action you ask a viewer to take, like “Comment your color.”

Draft — content that is being prepared and has NOT been published.

Reach — the number of accounts that saw your content, where available.

Engagement — interactions such as likes, comments, saves and shares.

How the 100-point editorial index works

Season relevance · 25

Official current/upcoming events score higher. Dates that need local verification get minimal credit.

Channel metric alignment · 20

Uses comparable, prior/current metrics for the selected goal. Views are not substituted for clicks or follows.

Post-level precedent · 20

A dated actual post on the same sport/channel is more informative than unrelated or missing examples.

Objective fit · 15

Whether the creative naturally serves your selected marketing objective; for website visits, verify a destination.

Product/content readiness · 10

Educate when stock is unknown. No unverified pricing, stock, partnerships or product-performance claims.

Calendar space · 10

Reduce priority when similar drafts already exist on that platform in the next seven days.

Confidence is separate from score. The index is a prioritization aid; a high score is not evidence of return on investment.
