RSH SPORTS MARKETING OS — artifact edition, build 25

24/7 Bots: Pixel, the Asset Bot
- A sixth bot at the start of the line. For each campaign it lists the files still needed
  (one photo, a 3 to 5 photo carousel set, a vertical video, optional extras per channel) and
  shows which are already attached.
- "Copy image prompt" and "Copy video brief" on every campaign give a ready-to-paste prompt for
  an AI image or video tool, built from the campaign's own visual direction. Every prompt carries
  the rules: sport-correct gear, no third-party logos, no real identifiable people, no text in
  the image or footage.
- Pixel does not generate images or video. A person makes the file in the tool, checks it, and
  drops it into the office; the rest of the team then runs.
