RSH Sports Marketing OS v8.5 — Finished Image Review

- Retains the beginner-friendly Smart Content Creator from v8.4.
- Attach a completed JPEG, PNG or WebP to a saved still-image post.
- Stores original uploaded images separately in the Mac app-support folder, not in browser localStorage.
- Previews the actual image and caption. Optional overlaid text is preview-only.
- Requires five human quality attestations before sending a still image to local manager review.
- Keeps all existing advanced modules and historical Metricool data.

Limitations: The app does not generate images, automatically inspect logos, verify rights, send approvals, or publish.
JSON browser backups do not include the image binary, only its Mac-local reference. Use copies of the original assets when migrating to a new computer.
