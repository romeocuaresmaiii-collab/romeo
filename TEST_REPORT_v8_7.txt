RSH Sports Marketing OS v8.7 — Stability Verification

REPRODUCED FAILURE AND FIX
1. Opening a workspace that already contained a saved campaign caused the startup quality audit to reference visualAssets before initialization. This caused a JavaScript startup exception and could make multiple buttons look unresponsive.
2. After fixing (1), an earlier Calendar renderer referenced SIMPLE_FORMATS before initialization. That also interrupted startup when posts existed.
3. Both initialization-order errors have now been fixed. The application also isolates non-critical screen render failures to avoid one widget disabling the others.
