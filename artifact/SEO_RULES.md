# Social SEO rules used by Quill (24/7 Bots)

Researched November 2026. Official platform pages where available; **[3P]** = third-party source; **[?]** = uncertain.
support.google.com could not be fetched directly from the build environment; YouTube items come from its official pages via search snippets.

## Instagram
- Search reads caption, hashtags, alt text, Name field and username. Comments are not searchable, so keywords go in the caption, not the first comment ([Sprout](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/instagram-seo/), [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-seo), [Later](https://later.com/blog/instagram-seo/)) [3P].
- Since 10 Jul 2025, public posts from professional accounts are indexed by Google. Stories are not ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-indexing-on-google/)).
- Primary keyword goes in the first ~55 characters. The feed cuts at about 125 characters; the Reels preview is shorter [?].
- **Max 5 hashtags per post** (Dec 2025). Hashtags categorize a post but do not drive reach ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/)).
- Write custom alt text: describe the image literally, keyword once, no stuffing.

## Facebook
- Search weights the opening caption lines and native captions [3P] ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/facebook-seo/)).
- 2–3 relevant hashtags. Meta cut reach for engagement bait and irrelevant hashtags (Apr 2025, [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/24/facebook-cracks-down-on-spammy-content-by-cutting-reach-and-monetization)) and demotes unoriginal reposts (Jul 2025, [Meta](https://creators.facebook.com/blog/combating-unoriginal-content)).

## TikTok
- Search reads caption, hashtags, on-screen text and speech [3P] ([Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-seo/)). Say and show the keyword in the first 3 seconds.
- **Max 5 hashtags** (in-app limit since mid-2025, [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/tiktok-limits-hashtags-460894)). Title 90 characters for posts made through schedulers.
- Keyword search ads launched in Jun 2025 ([TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-world-2025)). Search Center in Ads Manager is useful for keyword research.

## YouTube
- Title, description and thumbnail matter most; tags play a minimal role, mostly for misspellings ([YT Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/146402)).
- Title up to 100 characters ([YT Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57404)), keyword first (roughly 40–70 characters are visible).
- Description: keyword in the first 1–2 lines ([YT Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449)). Up to 3 hashtags show by the title; more than 60 and all are ignored ([YT Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658)).
- Since Jan 2026, search separates Shorts from long videos ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/01/09/youtube-search-filters-shorts-vs-long-form/)).

## Pinterest (not used for Tuesday DYK)
- Keywords in pin title, description, board title and destination URL ([Pinterest](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pin-performance-and-distribution)).
- Title 100 characters, keyword in the first 40. Description 500 characters [?]. No hashtags: keywords and topic tags instead.

## Threads
- One topic tag per post ([Instagram](https://about.instagram.com/blog/tips-and-tricks/instagram-threads-tips/)). Keyword in the opening sentence. 500 characters.

## Bluesky
- 1–3 hashtags that match active community feeds; always add alt text [3P] ([Sprout](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/bluesky-seo/)). 300 characters.

## Rules across all channels
1. One search-intent primary keyword per post (for example "custom wrestling singlets"), plus long-tail and US modifiers (high school, youth, club, varsity).
2. Seasonal terms from the sports calendar: wrestling Nov–Feb, basketball Nov–Mar, softball Feb–May, hockey Oct–Mar, soccer fall and spring.
3. Branded tag #RSHSports on every post; series tag #RSHDidYouKnow on Tuesday DYK videos. Hashtag mix: 1 broad, 2 niche, 1 branded.
4. Natural language, no stuffing, no bait ("comment YES", "like if", "tag 3 friends"), no #fyp, #viral or #explore.
5. Videos: keyword on screen and spoken in the first 3 seconds, captions burned in.
6. Approved captions are not rewritten. The keyword-first opening line is a suggestion that a person applies.
