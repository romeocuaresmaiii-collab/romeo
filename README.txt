RSH Sports Marketing OS v9.0 — Smart Growth Loop

Mac setup
1. Back up your current RSH Sports workspace in Settings & Backup.
2. Stop the previous app in Terminal with Control+C.
3. Extract this complete folder and run `python3 server.py` from inside it.
4. Open http://127.0.0.1:8765 in Chrome, then hard refresh with Cmd+Shift+R.
5. Look for App v9.0.0 · Connected in the header.

New in this release
- Create: three different editable audience-specific concepts from the same sports brief.
- Insights: dated platform-specific post comparisons with per-format medians and source links, including an optional manual comment-topic research log.
- Calendar & Reviews: local creative revision history showing caption changes, uploaded assets, and reviewer correction notes.
- Prompt enforcement: premium photorealism, accurate sport-specific apparel and equipment, and no third-party vendor logos on shoes, socks, uniforms, bags, or accessories.

Evidence limitations
- No automatic Metricool refresh inside this local app. Published source data are dated snapshots.
- Post comparison is descriptive, not a controlled A/B experiment or proof of causation.
- The app does not infer that a commenter is a parent, coach, or athlete.
- No automatic image generation or vendor-logo recognition.
- Local approvals are not authenticated; Anthony's approval must actually be received.
- No automatic publishing or commerce integration.

Media files live separately in your Mac's private RSH media storage. JSON backups include metadata, not video/image bytes; retain original files before changing computers.
