RSH SPORTS MARKETING OS — v9.5.0

Artifact edition: signed final approval
- The workspace owner registers one person as the final approver (Reviews, "Signed final approval").
- When that person presses Approve from their own Claude account, a sign-off is stored under their
  identity. The artifact's access rules let nobody else, the owner included, write or alter it.
- The sign-off carries a fingerprint of the campaign's text and attachments. If either changes
  afterwards, the signature stops counting and a new one is needed.
- The same conditions as before apply: manager review first, current media with five visual checks.
- The older typed-in approval still works and is labelled as not verified.
- Experiments can be filled from two published posts; campaigns can be linked to published posts.

Limits: a signature proves which Claude account approved, not who was at the keyboard. Anyone able
to republish the page could change the rules. Nothing is scheduled or published by an approval.
