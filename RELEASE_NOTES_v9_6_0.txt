RSH SPORTS MARKETING OS — v9.6.0

Artifact edition: safe for the whole team at once
- Before saving, the app reads the team's saved copy. If a teammate saved in the meantime, the two
  sets of changes are merged campaign by campaign and field by field, instead of one overwriting
  the other. Additions, deletions and log entries from both people are kept.
- Teammates' saves appear in an open page without reloading. The page waits until you have left
  the field you are typing in before redrawing.
- Opening the app with unsaved changes from an earlier visit merges them into the team copy.
- Only when two people change the same field of the same campaign does one value win (the later
  save). Attachments and signed approvals were already stored separately and are unaffected.
